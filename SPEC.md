# RC2000 Intake Lab

Reading Club 2000's public donation and inquiry forms inspire a shared hypothetical volunteer review desk. All rates, costs, controls, and events are synthetic. The experiment uses a finite M/M/c/K queue, an absorbing review chain, and a frequency-times-magnitude Monte Carlo loss model.

- Scenarios compares the original setup with two proposed interventions for each of three situations. Selections feed the Lab.
- Lab controls submissions per minute λ, reviews per volunteer per minute μ, staffed desks c, total intake capacity K, containment probability, and annual event frequency. K includes active reviews.
- Codex documents formulas, limitations, privacy considerations, and reproducible checks.
- The simulator runs independently in a browser. The only persisted value is the color theme.


The supplemental Lab models synthetic intake reference entropy, random review, rolling-window submission anomalies, and series/parallel availability. These are independent teaching models mapped to Modules 2 and 10, 7, 11, and 12.
